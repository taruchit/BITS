    d. nr_employed

  • Our target feature: "subscription" has strongest positive correlation with "duration". Thus, we can infer that as duration of marketing call increases, there is relatively higher likelihood of customer subscribing with the bank.

  • Our target feature: "subscription" has reltively weaker correlations with: -

    a. pdays -> If the number of days contacting client from previous campaign increases, there is relaltively lower likelihood of clients not subscribing with the bank.

    b. emp_var_rate -> If the employment variation rate increases, there is relatively lower likelihood of clients subscribing with the bank. This seems to be a logical indicator because higher employment variation rate means instability in economy and loss of jobs. Thus, if there are loss of jobs, people may not have enough funds to subscribe for term deposit.

    c. euribor3m -> If the euribor3m rate increases, there is relatively lower likelihood of clients subscribing with the bank for term deposit. This seems to be a logical indicator because lower euribor3m means borrowing become cheaper and thus, clients may have interest in using the remaining funds in term deposit. However, higher euribor3m rate means borrowing becomes expensive and thus, clients may not have funds to feel interested in subscribing for term deposit.

    d. nr_employed -> If the nr_employed increases, there is relatively lower likelihood of clients subscribing with the bank for term deposit. Thus, we observed inverse relation between the two features.

  • Heatmap was used as the visual to identify correlation between different numeric features and identify which set of features have strong correlation and which set of features have weak correlation.
  • Heatmap helps to visually represent the data and distinuish the strength of correlation using colors.
  • It helps to get summarized understanding of the relationship between all variables of interest.

Gestalt principles: -

  • Law of Similarity: -
a. When things appear similar to each other, we tend to group them together.

b. In our heatmap, we have used values for two shades: - purple and green. 

c. Purple gives color of negative correlations cells and their shades determine magnitude of correlation.

d. Green gives color of positive correlations cells and their shades determine magnitude of correlation.

e. Thus, the group of rows and columns which are of similar color gives easier interpretation of their correlation.

f. We can easily infer there are more cells with negative correlation than the cells with positive correlations.
  • Law of Pragnanz (Simplicity): -
a. When data is concise and simple, it easy to follow.

b. In our heatmap, we have kept precision value upto two decimal places. Thus, the data in each cell is readable, the cells are concise to read and interpret the data.    

c. We have kept x axis data horizontal and this, it can be easily read left to right.

d. We have added a color scale at the right hand side of the matrix to allow easier interpretation of colors with respect to correlation score between the features.

Preattentive attributes: -

  • Hue: -

    a. The intensity of each color helps us to show multiple correlationa values in the matrix effectively.

    b. Thus, as the reader of the visual we easily can infer the data from the chart based on the colors used and their relative shades.

  • Information Hierarchy: -
a. The magnitude of values is represnted by the shades of the colors used.
